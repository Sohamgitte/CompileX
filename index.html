Terms & Agreement

1. Pricing Policy

Project pricing is determined based on scope, complexity, and timeline. A detailed quote will be provided after our initial consultation. Prices are subject to change if project requirements significantly change from the original scope.

2. Payment Structure

  • 50% advance payment required before project commencement
  • Remaining 50% due upon project completion, before final delivery
  • For larger projects, milestone-based payments may be arranged
  • Payments are accepted via UPI, bank transfer, or other agreed methods

3. Revision Policy

  • Up to 2 rounds of minor revisions included in the base price
  • Major changes or scope additions will be quoted separately
  • Revision requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery
  • Design concept changes after approval may incur additional charges

4. Delivery Timeline

  • Estimated timelines are provided during project planning
  • Delays caused by late client feedback may extend the timeline
  • We'll communicate proactively about any schedule changes
  • Rush delivery may be available for an additional fee

5. Refund Policy

  • Advance payments are non-refundable once work has commenced
  • Partial refunds may be considered if project is cancelled before significant progress
  • No refunds after project delivery and final approval
  • Disputes will be resolved through mutual discussion

6. Communication Policy

  • Primary communication via email or agreed messaging platform
  • Response time: Within 24 hours on business days
  • Regular progress updates provided as per project schedule
  • Scheduled calls for important discussions and reviews

7. Intellectual Property

  • Full ownership of code and design transfers upon final payment
  • We reserve the right to showcase the project in our portfolio
  • Third-party assets (fonts, images) subject to their respective licenses
